Delete Your Account

RevEx  ·  Inefficient Code  ·  Last updated April 2026

RevEx is an app-review exchange platform by Inefficient Code. If you would like to delete your account and all associated data, follow the steps below.

How to request deletion

Option 1 — Email request (recommended)
Send an email to inefficientcode@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My RevEx Account". Include either your registered email address or your Firebase UID (visible in the app's profile screen). Your request will be processed within 30 days.
  1. Open the email link above (or compose a new email to inefficientcode@gmail.com).
  2. Use the subject line: Delete My RevEx Account.
  3. In the body, include your registered email address or Firebase UID so we can locate your account.
  4. Send the email. You will receive a confirmation reply within a few business days.
  5. Your account and data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of your request.

Partial deletion (data only)

If you only want specific data removed without deleting your full account — for example, removing a particular app listing or a submitted review — email inefficientcode@gmail.com with details of what you'd like removed and we will action it within 30 days.
