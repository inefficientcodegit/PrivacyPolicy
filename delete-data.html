Doomsday Trainer collects minimal anonymous data to enable leaderboards and matchmaking. This page explains what is stored and how to have it removed.

How to request data deletion

Email inefficientcode@gmail.com with the subject "Delete My Data — Doomsday Trainer". We will permanently delete all PlayFab records linked to your device within 30 days.

For Google Play Games data, go to your Google account → Security → Connected apps → Doomsday Trainer → Remove access.

To delete locally stored data, simply uninstall the app from your device.
