Privacy Policy

Doomsday Trainer  ·  Inefficient Code  ·  Last updated April 2026

Doomsday Trainer is a mental-arithmetic training game. This policy explains what data is collected, why, and how you can have it removed. We collect the minimum necessary to provide analytics, leaderboards and matchmaking — no advertising, no tracking, no personal information.

1. What we collect and why

DataPlatformStored whereWhy
Anonymous usage events (screen views, game starts, match completions) iOS · Android · Web Firebase Analytics (Google) Helps us understand how the app is used so we can improve it. No name, email or advertising ID is ever linked to these events.
Anonymous device identifier iOS · Android PlayFab (Microsoft Azure) Creates and identifies your anonymous game account for leaderboards and 1v1 matchmaking. No name, email or password is ever required.
Game stats (Elo rating, wins, match count, average answer time) iOS · Android PlayFab (Microsoft Azure) Powers global leaderboards and skill-based matchmaking.
Ghost match recording (your answer timings from a completed match) iOS · Android PlayFab (Microsoft Azure) Used as a ghost opponent for other players when no live match is available.
Generated display name (e.g. "SwiftSolver42") iOS · Android PlayFab (Microsoft Azure) Shown on leaderboards. Randomly generated — not your real name.
Leaderboard scores and cloud save (game progress) Android Google Play Games (Google) Optional. Enables cloud backup and cross-device progress sync. Only activated if you connect Play Games in Settings.
Game Center authentication iOS Apple Game Center Optional. Enables global leaderboards via Apple's platform if you are signed in to Game Center in iOS Settings.
Local game progress (streaks, scores, settings) iOS · Android · Web Your device only Stored locally in the app. Never transmitted. Deleted when you uninstall the app.

2. What we do NOT collect

  • Name, email address, phone number or any contact information
  • Location data
  • Camera or microphone access
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA / GAID)
  • Crash logs or diagnostic telemetry
  • Browsing history or data from other apps
  • Payment or financial information

There are no adverts and no in-app purchases. Analytics data is fully anonymous and used only to improve the app — it is never used for advertising or sold to third parties.

3. Third-party services

Firebase Analytics (Google) iOS · Android · Web — collects anonymous usage events such as screen views and game interactions. No name, email, advertising ID or personal information is linked to these events. Data is retained for approximately 14 months per Google's standard policy. firebase.google.com/support/privacy

PlayFab (Microsoft Azure) iOS · Android — hosts anonymous player accounts, leaderboards, and matchmaking. privacy.microsoft.com

Google Play Games Android only — optional cloud save and leaderboard service. policies.google.com/privacy

Apple Game Center iOS only — optional leaderboard service. apple.com/legal/privacy

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with advertisers or any third party beyond the services listed in section 3. Your anonymous device identifier and game stats are shared with PlayFab solely to provide in-game features. Analytics data is shared with Google solely to provide Firebase Analytics.

5. Data retention

Firebase Analytics data is retained for approximately 14 months. Your PlayFab account and associated game data are retained for as long as the app is in active use. If you request deletion, your PlayFab data will be permanently removed within 30 days.

6. Your rights — deleting your data

  • PlayFab account & game stats: email inefficientcode@gmail.com with subject "Delete My Account — Doomsday Trainer". Processed within 30 days. Or visit: Delete Account page
  • Firebase Analytics: opt out via your device's standard ad/analytics settings, or use a network-level blocker.
  • Google Play Games data: Google account Settings → Connected apps → Doomsday Trainer → Remove access.
  • Local device data: Uninstall the app.

7. Children

Doomsday Trainer does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app contains no advertising, no social features, and no in-app purchases. All accounts are fully anonymous.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The current version is always available at this URL. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
