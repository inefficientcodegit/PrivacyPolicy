Delete Account — Doomsday Trainer

You can request deletion of your Doomsday Trainer account and all associated data. Accounts are anonymous — no email or password is ever created — but your game stats and match history are stored on our backend.

What gets deleted

Submitting a request will permanently delete:

  • Your anonymous PlayFab account
  • All game stats (Elo, wins, match history)
  • Your ghost match recording
  • Your leaderboard entries

Google Play Games cloud saves and leaderboard scores are managed separately by Google — see below.

How to request deletion

Option 1 — Email us Send an email to inefficientcode@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My Account — Doomsday Trainer". We will process your request within 30 days.
Option 2 — Google Play Games data Open your device's Google account settings → Connected apps → Doomsday Trainer → Remove access. This revokes cloud save access and removes Play Games leaderboard data.

Retention period

After a verified request, all PlayFab data is deleted within 30 days. Aggregate anonymised statistics (e.g. total match counts) that cannot be linked to any individual may be retained.
