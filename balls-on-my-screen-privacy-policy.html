Balls on my Screen

Privacy Policy · Last updated: May 2026

Balls on my Screen ("the app", "we") is a number-matching puzzle game developed by Inefficient Code. This policy describes what data the app collects and how it is used.

1. Data We Collect

The app does not collect any personal data. All game state, statistics, and preferences are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted to any server.

2. Local Storage

The following information is stored locally using your device's standard storage (Capacitor Preferences / localStorage):

  • Game settings (sound, music, vibration, dark mode)
  • Game statistics (scores, games played, balls cleared)
  • Saved game state (so you can resume a game)
  • Current game mode preference

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Name, email address, or any contact information
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Analytics or usage events
  • Crash reports
  • Payment or billing information
  • Photos, camera, or microphone
  • Contacts or social graph

4. Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or cloud backends. No data is shared with any third party.

5. Network Access

The app does not make any network requests. It functions entirely offline.

6. Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13. It contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no online features.

7. Data Deletion

To delete all locally stored data, simply uninstall the app from your device.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
