Privacy Policy

Puzzoku  ·  Inefficient Code  ·  Last updated: April 2026

Puzzoku is an arithmetic logic puzzle game built by Inefficient Code. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

Local Game progress & settings

Your level progress, best times, achievement history, daily streak, and app settings are stored locally on your device only. On iOS this uses Apple's UserDefaults (via the Capacitor Preferences API); on Android it uses SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

Analytics Anonymous usage data (Firebase Analytics)

We use Firebase Analytics (Google) to collect anonymous, aggregated data about how the app is used. This includes:

  • When a game is started or completed, and the grid size chosen
  • Time taken to complete a puzzle
  • When a hint is used, and the grid size at the time
  • When the tutorial is started, completed, or exited
  • The platform the app is running on (iOS or Android)

No personally identifiable information (name, email, location, device ID) is collected. Data is used solely to understand feature usage and improve the app. You can review Google's data practices at policies.google.com/privacy.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

  • No name, email address, or contact information
  • No precise or approximate location
  • No camera, microphone, or contacts access
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties for marketing

3. No Ads or In-App Purchases

Puzzoku is a one-time download with no advertisements and no in-app purchases. There is nothing to buy and no ad network involved.

4. Children's Privacy

Puzzoku does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app contains no ads, no in-app purchases, and no social features. Anonymous analytics data described above does not include any information that could identify an individual user.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Local game data can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the app. Anonymous Firebase Analytics data is retained by Google for up to 14 months per their standard policy. We do not have a way to identify or delete individual anonymous records.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
